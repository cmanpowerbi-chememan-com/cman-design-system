The Chememan Story

From a single kiln in Saraburi to Southeast Asia's most trusted lime supplier.

We didn't set out to be the biggest. We set out to be the cleanest, the most reliable, and the most chemistry-honest lime producer in the region — and 35 years later, that focus is what every customer trades on.

Founded in 1989 with a single rotary kiln, Chememan now operates five plants across Thailand, exporting to twelve countries and serving sectors as diverse as steel, water treatment, sugar refining, semiconductor manufacturing, and pulp & paper.

Our roadmap to 2030 is built on three commitments: net-negative carbon lime production, AI-monitored kiln operations at every site, and full traceability from limestone quarry to customer delivery — the same chemistry, but accountable end-to-end.
